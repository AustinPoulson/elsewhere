# Expedition 012 — Glass limpets

## New ecological capability

The glass limpet is a fixed shoreline capture feeder. In a suitable pool, real loose pigment pays for a shell. A rainbell can spend its own pigment to pump an ordinary current; a coarse grain carried past the shell can then be intercepted into separate reserve and catch stores. That color is temporarily unavailable to a downstream grazer. The shell later returns its body and held color as ordinary pigment that grazers can gather. The visitor can watch the sequence without using a tool. Some worlds support no limpets, and neither the opening nor migrated world receives a free one.

| Condition or transfer  | Implemented policy                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Establishment          | One rotating source-ID anchor in the normalized shoreline band 0.65–0.9, with 14-unit coast margin, must be a coarse grain of strength at least 0.12. Grains within 32 units pay exactly 0.3 in distance/ID order: body 0.2 and reserve 0.1. Other shells stay at least 64 units away. Four per type and 24 active new-type bodies overall bound presence. A failed poll advances only its saved source-ID cursor.                                                                                                               |
| Food and flow          | A live coarse grain within 22 units can feed the shell only while its actual current-derived sediment velocity is at least 0.5 world units/second. The velocity uses the existing three-field sum, combined clamp 16 and sediment scale 0.35. Intake is at most 0.04 pigment/second; 25% reaches reserve and 75% reaches catch, clipped against both 0.2/0.8 capacities before the exact grain debit. The shell stays fixed.                                                                                                     |
| Cost, decline and exit | Maintenance dissipates at most 0.001 reserve/second into a named ledger. Calm water, scarce grain or a full store stops intake; reserve can run out. At age 480 seconds or 60 continuous seconds with zero reserve, the body, remaining reserve and catch return once through the common one-grain/nearest-underfilled-grain/mineralization rule. The slot is freed. Later return to the catalog requires a fresh funded opening; there is no reproduction, larva, dormant inventory or retained corpse in this lifecycle slice. |

This is a local, dissipative food-retention relationship, not a claim that the whole ecosystem has a closed resource balance or stable equilibrium.

## Playable before and after

The production build was played at normal rates in fresh Chrome contexts at 1280×800 desktop and 390×844 portrait. The disposable starting world had a previously paid rainbell, one coarse grain, one ordinary grazer and no limpet. The new shell appeared by ecological admission near world second 37 after its grain paid 0.3. The rainbell paid 0.04 for an actual current; the grain moved through the shell's reach; the shell's visible catch and saved reserve rose by an exact source debit. A paired equal-input world with limpet admission deferred retained zero shells and zero limpet intake. At about world second 41.2, grazer cargo plus nest color was 0.519 in the limpet world versus 0.595 in the desktop control and 0.697 in the portrait control. The shell was holding part of the difference. The calm-current unit control had zero limpet intake from an otherwise adjacent grain.

A valid near-exit checkpoint avoided waiting eight real minutes: after the shell's finite return, an unchanged grazer gathered its released ordinary pigment in both layouts. This proves the return path and reavailability, while the complete 480-second lifespan is covered in simulation tests. The observed normal-rate opening, moving grain, catch, no-type control, later return and grazer pickup are separate from the synthetic capacity fixture. Screenshots and machine-readable samples are in the ignored local `.artifacts/glass-limpet-20261026/` folder. Pointer and arrow-key/Enter Observe, exact pause, snapshot export/import, legacy import, reset cancellation/confirmation, study-world restoration, portrait fit, reduced-motion initial pause/resume and no page errors passed in disposable browsers. Physical touch-device behavior was not verified.

## Shared founding and bounded work (supporting work)

The linked [catalog-scale founding budget](https://trello.com/c/1RetpYmP) is necessary side work for the fifth installed new type; it is not a second new organism. `world/species/founding.ts` now rotates exactly one normal founding type per six ticks. Refusals and absent types advance that saved type cursor without resource, ID, PRNG or successful-birth-budget debit. A saved skimmer source-ID cursor now advances past a crowded paid nest so another home can be tried; the other types retain their own bounded candidate cursors. One saved `nextFoundingTick` permits at most one successful body allocation per six ticks across normal admissions and accepted off-phase event offers. The event API reserves this accounting path for a future death-founded organism; this release adds no such population or corpse queue. Empty types skip their target maps. Sediment-admission neighborhoods are built in one bounded full source pass, with selected local payment lookup. Glass limpet candidates rotate by source ID even if valid imported arrays are reordered.

All five types share 24 active records, despite per-type caps summing to 26. At four reusable objects per body, new-type rendering is at most 96 objects plus the existing 16 short cues; limpets add no cues, particle history or trails. The existing 42 lucents, eight grazers, 16 nests, 32 lights, 600 grains, three currents and 14 trail points/lucent remain bounded. A synthetic mixed maximum of all these collections plus 24 new-type bodies ran for 20 simulated minutes at the production 1/30-second step, with three renewed currents and exact save continuation at 10 minutes. This stress fixture explicitly renews resources and ages to hold capacity; it is a work and persistence test, not evidence of naturally funded coexistence.

## Persistence and verification

World version 3 advances from schema revision 6 to 7. Pure migration adds an empty limpet population, neutral admission deadline, zero limpet/skimmer source-ID and type cursors, an available founding budget and zero limpet ledgers. Existing paid organisms, resources, IDs, time, weather, currents and PRNG state are preserved. Strict imports check shoreline geometry and spacing, stores, admission/source/budget bounds, aggregate counts, unique global IDs and live reference types. A removed grain may remain a saved stale target; it is safely cleared on continuation without a debit. Old-schema hybrids and malformed current saves reject. The historical shower-study JSON remains unchanged.

`npm ci` succeeded. Final `npm run validate` passed 176 tests in 24 files, TypeScript and the production Vite build. Regression checks cover paid appearance, source-ID rotation and reordered imports, current-gated exact intake, calm and stale-target refusals, both store caps, full-floor finite return, arbitrary-phase save continuation including a vanished target, shared founding fairness (including crowded skimmer homes), one-pass source scans and malformed root spacing, and the 20-minute mixed maximum. `npm audit --omit=dev` reported zero production advisories. Dev-only Vitest toolchain advisories remain outside this feature's runtime scope.

## Side fixes and limits

No independent bug card was changed. Historical fixture builders were updated for schema 7, and the limpet Observe wording was made neutral in calm water; these are necessary compatibility and accuracy work, not additional ecological features. There is no limpet reproduction or dormant stage. Browser QA was emulated on desktop and portrait viewports; no physical touch device or long-running real-browser soak was verified.
