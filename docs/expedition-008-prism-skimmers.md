## New organism and ecological role

A translucent diamond-shaped recycler emerges from a nest that can pay0.6 pigment, keeps0.2 in its body and0.4 in reserve, gathers loose grains and converts0.9 reserve into1.35 ordinary feeding light. Lucents consume that light; grazers compete for the same sediment. No visitor action is required. At most six skimmers occupy the proposed shared24 new-type slots; one arrival per sixty world seconds, one per home and48-unit spacing preserve variable presence. New and migrated worlds start with none. A fresh funded opportunity permits later return.

Reserve maintenance costs0.002pigment/sec. Sixty continuous seconds without reserve or eight minutes of life ends the animal. Its actual remaining body/reserve transfers to one grain, or tops up the nearest underfilled grain; any excess is recorded as mineralization. Saturated floors cannot delay death or evict unrelated sediment. This slice deliberately does not implement reproduction, inheritance, dormant propagules or globally conserved ecology. Existing lucents and grazers remain immortal.
