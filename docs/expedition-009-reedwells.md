## New organism and ecological purpose

Rain intensity at least0.5 permits one legal grain-centered pocket to pay exactly0.6 pigment from sources within32 units, ordered by distance then ID. No seed or resource is gifted. Leaves unfold around the paid root, catch external daylight, brighten with stored energy and release ordinary light that lucents actually consume. Grazers and skimmers compete for the same loose pigment that could fund roots. Natural/called rain opens establishment opportunities; daylight independently controls charging. A pool can remain plant-free.

Each plant accepts at most0.18 light energy/second times daylight, stores at most3, and debits exactly1.5 into one ordinary light after at least15 seconds since its previous release. Full light capacity retains charge; offered overflow is named unharvested daylight rather than future debt. Night stops harvesting, while an existing store may still release. This is declared external energy, not pigment conversion or a claim of closed-world conservation. The existing paid lucent feeding correction remains unchanged.

At900 world seconds the root returns its actual0.6 pigment through the shared bounded death rule. At most one grain is created or nearest underfilled grain topped up; every unplaced remainder is named mineralization. Stored light dissipates into its separate ledger field. The body disappears and frees its population slot. Another root requires new wet funding and the admission rest. This slice deliberately has no reproductive seed, inherited traits or dormant plant stage, and does not promise equilibrium or that six roots sustain24 lucents. Existing lucents/grazers retain their existing immortality.
