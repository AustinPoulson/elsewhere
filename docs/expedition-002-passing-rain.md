# Expedition 002: a passing rain

2026-10-09. Local implementation and acceptance: **verified**. The PR discussion records the final publication receipt and deployed commit; the local copy is retained under `.artifacts/`.

## Relationship

Rain briefly spreads the visitor's light across the water. Lucents feed farther from a patch's center and orbit a wider area, leaving their usual sediment across more floor. The existing grazers gather those traces into their woven nests. Rain changes the food chain without adding free light, energy, or a new collectible.

Each three-minute cycle begins with 30 seconds of calm, followed by a 38-second shower. Six-second eased transitions expand feeding reach from 64 to 112 world units and back; the preferred orbit expands in the same proportion. Ringlets on the water and visibly broader warm halos show that relationship. The observations include a rain countdown, and the field guide explains the passing shower.

## Limits and persistence

Weather derives entirely from the saved ecological clock. Snapshots remain version 2, the autosave key stays unchanged, and the existing version 1 grazer migration continues to preserve old worlds. Pause holds weather and the ecosystem together; hidden pages do not catch up later. The renderer reuses one masked graphics object with 48 deterministic ringlet slots, with no particle history or additional persisted entities. Reduced motion holds ringlet geometry still. Existing species, light, sediment, nest, and fixed-step budgets remain unchanged. No dependencies were added or changed.

## Limits and next observations
