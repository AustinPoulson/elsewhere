## New capability

Stir water (S) turns a short directional stroke into an eight-world-second current. Lucents, grazers and loose pigment drift along it; light and nests stay anchored. The same existing food can now meet a different creature. Keyboard visitors mark the start with Enter, move with arrows and commit with Enter; Escape cancels. A tap is a refusal, not a current.

Up to three currents overlap. Each has a110-unit radial influence with linear spatial and lifetime falloff,12-unit/second peak force and16 combined clamp. Sediment follows at0.35. Creature swimming velocity is unchanged. The current owns no energy or pigment; ordinary feeding and fading retain their accounts. Strokes are24–240 units and coast-tested with33 bounded samples, including the path across inlets. Full capacity refuses without evicting an existing field or changing IDs.
