# Expedition 013 — Cargo mites

## New ecological capability

The cargo mite is a mobile parasite of pigment grazers. A sufficiently loaded grazer pays for a founder from its own carried color. The mite stays with the grazer, draws only from actual cargo, and can pay for a child from its own reserve. This makes a visible, funded family that competes with the nest for a grazer's food. Existing grazer movement, feeding and nest delivery continue autonomously; no visitor treatment or placement control was added.

| Condition or transfer     | Implemented policy                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Founding                  | The shared six-tick catalog scheduler tries one host ID at a time. Cargo at least 0.5 pays exactly 0.5: body 0.1, reserve 0.4. Only an unoccupied host can found a mite, with 48-unit spacing between independent host sites. A successful founder rests this type for 60 seconds. A failed candidate only advances its saved host-ID cursor. Four mites per type and 24 active new-type bodies overall bound population.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Feeding and host movement | An attached mite follows its real host after locomotion and after current drift; it never takes that drift twice. It draws at most 0.008 pigment/second from host cargo into a reserve capped at 0.5. Maintenance dissipates at most 0.0005 reserve/second into a named ledger. When cargo empties, the grazer's returning flag clears atomically so it can forage again. A missing host leaves the mite at its last position; a staggered search considers at most eight loaded grazers within 80 units, then it approaches at at most eight units/second before attaching. There is no teleport to a distant host. |
| Paid child                | At age 30 or later, with reserve at least 0.4, a free second host slot, safe ID, per-type/global capacity, the saved shared successful-birth budget and its own deadline, a parent transfers exactly 0.2 reserve to a child's 0.1 body and 0.1 reserve. The first deadline is birth plus 30 seconds; later births wait 60 seconds. A refused birth allocates and pays nothing. Juveniles immediately count against both caps and can feed from the same host. No egg bank, lineage log or inherited trait exists in this slice.                                                                                      |
| Decline and exit          | No host cargo means no intake. After 240 seconds of age or 60 continuous seconds with zero reserve, the body and remaining reserve return once through the common bounded pigment-return rule; excess at a saturated floor mineralizes. The slot is freed. Return to the population needs a new paid founder or child, and a type definition alone never creates a body.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Body pigment, reserve and host cargo are separate accounted stores. The legacy grazer remains an immortal carrier under the current contract; this release does not claim a closed ecosystem or grazer mortality.

## Playable before and after

The production build was played at normal rates in disposable Chrome contexts at 1280×800 desktop and 390×844 portrait. A loaded ordinary grazer carried 1.2 pigment toward its nest. In the positive world, a new mite appeared near world second 37 after a 0.5 cargo debit and fed from the remaining bundle. The otherwise identical no-mite world deferred founding without changing its starting resources. Near world second 41.5, the positive nest held about 0.663 pigment while the no-mite nest held about 1.200. The mite was holding paid material and had taken additional cargo; the difference was not a new resource. Both viewports showed the tiny attached body, and pointer plus arrow-key/Enter Observe selected that same displayed position.

A separate pigment-rich host world showed paid parent-to-child transfer at world second 66.4 on desktop and 66.5 on portrait. The young mite continued feeding and passed age 30 in both layouts; its reserve had risen to about 0.326 by world second 96.4. A valid late-life checkpoint showed removal and return at ordinary speed, with returned pigment gathered into the existing nest. Source and return tests cover exact accounting, full floors and refusal paths. Machine-readable samples and screenshots are under the ignored local `.artifacts/cargo-mite-20261028/` folder. These are disposable test worlds; no personal save was read or replaced.

Pause held the world exactly. Snapshot export/import continued the saved host/child state, the historical shower save migrated with no mite or pigment grant, reset cancellation and confirmation worked, and the study visit restored the personal test world exactly. Desktop and portrait had no page errors or horizontal overflow. Reduced-motion portrait began paused and produced a funded feeding mite only after resume. Physical touch hardware was not tested.

## Bounds, persistence and verification

World version 3 advances schema revision 7 to 8. Pure migration adds an empty mite array, an inactive admission clock and host cursor, and zero intake/maintenance ledgers. All prior resources, IDs, ages, time, weather, currents and PRNG state remain. Strict import validates host/parent/target IDs and kinds, attached versus seeking state, host sync, deadlines, body and reserve bounds, per-host/type/aggregate counts and global IDs. Historical save revisions remain accepted while hybrids and malformed current worlds reject. No prior world gains free mites.

The catalog's one normal search and one successful new-body allocation per six ticks now includes mite founders and offspring. The per-host two-mite cap is inside the same four-per-type and 24-total cap. Seeking searches at most eight host candidates on a staggered six-tick cadence; absent mites allocate no target cache. Rendering uses three reused objects per mite, at most 12 for this type and under the aggregate 96-object allowance, with no new cues, trails or unbounded history. A synthetic 20-minute mixed-capacity run included mites within 24 new-type bodies alongside 42 lucents, eight grazers, 16 nests, 32 lights, 600 grains and three currents, with exact save continuation at ten minutes. Its explicit resource/age renewal holds worst-case workload and is not a claim of natural equilibrium.

`npm ci` and final `npm run validate` passed: 188 tests in 27 files, TypeScript and the production Vite build. `npm audit --omit=dev` found zero production advisories. Focused checks cover exact host debit and returning-mode normalization, loaded-host admission rotation, host spacing, shared birth refusal, parent-child transfer, real feeding and maintenance, same-host cap, host loss without teleport, finite exit on a full floor, old/current save validation, a coast-edge host save, a visible shore-edge mite, and a normal-rate child that feeds and matures. Existing historical-fixture builders were updated for schema 8 without weakening rejection tests.

## Side fixes and limits

No independent bug card was changed. Schema-fixture updates, the grazer returning-mode normalization and two review-found shore-edge fixes are necessary parts of this organism's accounting/visibility, not separate daily features. Mites have no inherited traits or free dormant eggs; old lucents and grazers retain their existing lifecycle scope. Browser QA used viewport emulation and disposable contexts; a physical touch device and sustained real-browser soak were not verified.
